The Truth: LIC vs. Bank Fixed Deposits vs. Mutual Funds

Clear, honest financial facts every policyholder must understand before investing.

Section 37 Sovereign Guarantee

Backed by the Central Government

Bank fixed deposits are insured only up to ₹5 Lakhs by DICGC. In contrast, under Section 37 of the LIC Act 1956, the Government of India guarantees every single rupee of your sum assured and declared bonuses in cash. Zero default risk.

100% Capital & Profit Protection

Real Tax Freedom (80C & 10(10D))

Zero Tax on Death & Qualified Maturity

Bank FD interest is taxed at your income tax slab (up to 31.2%), reducing your real return to 4-5%. LIC policies give Section 80C deductions up to ₹1.5L, and death claims are 100% tax-free without ceiling. Maturity proceeds for policies with annual premium up to ₹5L are completely tax-free under Section 10(10D).

Keep 100% of Your Compounding Gains

Day 1 Family Protection

Human Life Value (HLV)

If you save ₹50,000 in a mutual fund or FD and pass away after 6 months, your family gets only what you deposited. In an LIC plan, paying just one installment immediately creates a ₹10,00,000+ safety net that ensures your children's education and family lifestyle continue uninterrupted.
